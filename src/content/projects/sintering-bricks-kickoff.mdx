We're starting a hands-on research program to close the loop from loose Martian
regolith simulant to a working thermal-storage structure, using concentrated
sunlight as the only energy input.

**Initial plan:**

1. **Feedstock characterization** — sieve MGS-1 into particle-size fractions and
   record baseline mineralogy and moisture content.
2. **Mirror rig** — build/calibrate a parabolic mirror mount that can hold a
   stable focal spot on a brick mold, and measure the achievable focal
   temperature across the day.
3. **First sinter attempts** — vary compaction pressure and dwell time, and
   document each firing on video.

Results, videos, and data will be posted here as log entries.
