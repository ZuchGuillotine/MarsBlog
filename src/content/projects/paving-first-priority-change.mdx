**Decision.** The first product is a paved surface, not a brick. Paving is one
continuous process with no molds or handling, and it has a clear first use:
landing pads, aprons and short haul roads that stop rocket-plume ejecta and dust.

**Why now.** ESA's PAVER project (2023) showed that melting fine simulant into
glass tiles works but is slow, shallow (~2 cm), glassy and prone to cracking.
Our approach melts coarse rock, stirs in preheated fines to form a crystal mush,
and anneals it under a reflective hood. The physics case is on the project page.

**What changes in V1.**

1. Feed changes from sieved MGS-1 powder to crushed Columbia River basalt in
   fine and coarse beds, with LMS-1, LHS-1 and MGS-1 kept for validation runs.
2. New instruments: an LWIR camera on the cooling wake, a load cell on a manual
   rake, and the iris "blink-and-read" pyrometry mode.
3. New variables: bed type, preheat temperature, filler fraction and filler
   temperature, hood on/off.
4. The experiment record template now includes these fields.

**Status.** V1 hardware is in procurement. No test data yet.
